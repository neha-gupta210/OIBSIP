# Retail Sales Data Analysis

## Objective
The Objective of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data and identify useful patterns and insights related to sales, customers, product categories, and payment methods.

## Dataset
The dataset contains 1000 retail sales records with information such as:
- Order ID
- Date
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Payment Method
- City
- Store Type
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount
- Delivery Status

  ## Analysis Performed
  The following analysis was performed:
  - Initial data inspection
  - Missing value checking
  - Descriptive statistical analysis
  - Monthly sales analysis
  - Quarterly sales analysis
  - Age group distribution
  - Gender distribution
  - Quantity sold by product category
  - Revenue by product category
  - Correlation heatmap
  - Payment method distribution

    ## Key Findings
    - May 2023 recorded the highest monthly sales.
    - Q2 and Q4 showed strong quarterly sales performance.
    - The 46-55 age group had the highest number of customers.
    - Female customers were slightly higher than male customers.
    - Clothing had the highest quantity sold.
    - Electronics generated the highest revenue.
    - Debit Card and mobile payment were the most commonly used payment methods.
    - Price per unit showed a strong positive relationship with total amount.
   
      ## Business Recommendations
      1. Focus marketing campaigns on the 46- 55 age group.
      2. Give special attention to Electronics because it generates the highest revenue.
      3. Maintain convenient payment option such as Debit Card and Mobile Payment.
      4. Plan promotional campaigns during periods with higher sales performance.
     
      ## Dataset Limitation
      The dataset contains product category information but does not contain individual product names. Therefore, product-       level Top 10 analysis could not be performed using this dataset.
